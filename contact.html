faqs

Are your products 100% organic?

Answer:

Yes — all Aura Organic products are made using certified organic ingredients sourced from trusted farms that follow sustainable growing practices.

Do you offer delivery?

Answer:

We currently deliver to all major cities within Egypt, with international shipping available for select products. Delivery times range from 2-5 business days, depending on your location.

How should I store the drinks and yogurts?

Answer:

Our superfood drinks and yogurts are best kept refrigerated at 2-5°C. Avoid exposure to direct sunlight or heat to maintain freshness and flavor.

Can I return or exchange an item?

Answer:

We accept returns for unopened products within 7 days of purchase. Please reach out through our contact form or email support@auraorganic.com for assistance.

Are your bottles and packaging recyclable?

Answer:

Absolutely — our bottles are made from 100% recyclable PET plastic, and we’re moving toward biodegradable packaging across all product lines.

Are your energy boosters safe for daily use?

Answer:

Yes, when consumed in moderation. Each blend is formulated with natural plant extracts and no artificial caffeine — designed to uplift without the crash.

Where can I find Aura Organic products in stores?

Answer:

Our products are available in select health stores, cafés, and organic markets. You can also find nearby locations using our Store Locator page.

How can I collaborate or become a distributor?

Answer:

We’re always open to partnerships that share our mission. Please contact our business team at partners@auraorganic.com for inquiries.
